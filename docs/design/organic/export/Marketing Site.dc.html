Four ways to start a recipe

Bring one in

Paste the text, drop a screenshot or share a link. Recipe Lab turns it into a clean recipe you can cook and change.

Describe it

Say what you want in plain words, like "a quick weeknight dal". Recipe Lab writes the full recipe.

Branch an existing one

Start from any recipe in Recipe Lab and change what you need. The original stays untouched.

Start from your fridge

List what you have. See recipes that use all of it, or have one written around it.

Branch with AI

Change a recipe in one sentence.

"Less sugar." "Dairy-free." "Make it in one tray." Tap a quick change or type your own and Recipe Lab rewrites the ingredients and method to match.

Family tree

Every version, in one place.

See where a recipe started and every way it's been changed since. Switch between a tree, a timeline and a map, and jump to any version.

What changed

Know exactly what's different.

Compare any two versions line by line. Added, removed and changed ingredients are marked, and only the steps that changed are shown.

For families

Suggest a fix instead of a comment.

Tap the line you'd change and send it to the author. They can apply it in one tap, or keep it as a branch with your name on it.

Scale servings

Cooking for two or twelve. Every amount updates as you go.
