## Context

The Settings Account card (RL-093, RL-165) shows the avatar and name. 13a adds the email with
the provider and a Sign-in methods row. Firebase exposes `user.providerData` (`password` is not
used; email link shows as `emailLink` sign-in method under the `password` provider id, Google as
`google.com`).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Under the name: "<email> · Google" when `google.com` is linked, "<email> · Email link" otherwise; nothing for anonymous accounts.
- [ ] A row "Sign-in methods" with the count at the right (1 or 2), not interactive in this ticket.
- [ ] `AppUser` gains `providers: ('google' | 'email')[]` mapped in `AuthContext`.
- [ ] Tests for the three cases.
- [ ] `docs/capabilities.md` Settings mention.
- [ ] A `backlog/QA.md` entry (live after RL-191).
- [ ] `npm test`, `npm run lint`, `npm run build` pass.

## Out of scope

Linking or unlinking methods from Settings.

## Verification

Component tests.
