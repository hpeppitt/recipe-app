## Context

The author reversed decision 3 for stored photos on 2026-10-20, starting with the Phase 1 cook
photo: optional on "I made this", and it "becomes the recipe photo, washed and credited, if the
author has none". Decision 3 says a reversal "is a phase of its own, not a field" and needs
Storage, upload UX and a moderation answer. Facts that shape the answer:
- Cloud Storage for Firebase needs the Blaze plan, and moving to Blaze ends the free Gemini tier
  (docs/operations.md ~422; decision 2).
- Decision 3's reason against base64 in Firestore is the 1 MiB document limit and read
  bandwidth for food photography. A washed photo shown at 390x230 (780x460 at 2x) is about
  60 to 120 KB as JPEG at quality 0.7.
- `services/imageDownscale.ts` already downscales in the browser (RL-118).
- No recipe has a photo today, so "if the author has none" is always true at first.
- Reporting and moderation tooling is cut (private circle, decision 1).
RL-204 to RL-208 are written for the recommendations and are regroomable.

## Questions and the groomer's recommendation

1. **Where**: (a) Firestore, a separate collection `cookPhotos/{cookId}` holding one data URL
   per cook, kept out of the cook and recipe documents so lists never pull image bytes
   (recommended: no bill, rules-enforced, same pattern as uploaded avatars, RL-019);
   (b) Cloud Storage on Blaze (ends the free Gemini tier; against decision 2);
   (c) no photo in Phase 1.
2. **Size**: downscaled in the browser to a long side of 800px, JPEG, at most 150 KB
   (about 200000 base64 characters), enforced in the rules (recommended).
3. **Who can add**: the cook, verified, only on their own cook, once (recommended).
4. **Removal (moderation)**: the cook can delete their photo; the recipe owner can delete any
   photo on their recipe; nothing automated (recommended). The photo picker says "Food only,
   please. No people or personal details." like the chat photo notice (RL-119/120).
5. **Becoming the recipe photo**: derived, not written: the recipe page shows the most recent
   remaining cook photo, washed, with "Photo by <name>" (recommended). Nothing writes to the
   recipe document, so no foreign-write rule is needed. Explore tiles and search rows stay emoji
   in Phase 1 (a per-tile read would multiply feed cost).
6. **Visibility**: public read, like the recipe (recommended).
7. **Data rights**: photos join Download my data and the erase script (recommended).
8. **Privacy policy**: a photo paragraph drafted in RL-208, signed off by the author.
