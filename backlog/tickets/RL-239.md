## Context

A new route that signs people in and changes data, hence tier hard. The sign-in sheet
(`AuthModal`, `AuthAction`) takes a new action `{ kind: 'join-kitchen'; name }` ("Sign in to join
Hana's kitchen", `Users` icon). The email link returns to the app origin, not the page
(capabilities); RL-135/136 add a return path; until then, after sign-in the user taps the link
again (say so on the sheet's sent step).

### The design (the implementer has no other copy)

- Kitchen header: to the right of the title, a stack of 30px member avatars (-8px overlap, 2px
  ground ring); under the title 13px `neutral-700` "Shared with Ola and Sami" (or "Just you").
  A 36px secondary pill "Invite" (`UserPlus`) opens the share sheet with the link (Web Share API,
  copy fallback) and the line "Anyone with this link can join for 7 days."
- Pantry header: the same avatar stack in a pill.
- Owner: a "People" row in Kitchen's More menu lists members with Remove; a member sees "Leave
  this kitchen" (19a confirm).
- `/join/:code`: outside `AppShell`; a sheet over a plain ground background: 84px blob with
  `CookingPot`, Caprasimo 24px "Join Hana's kitchen", 14px "Share a shopping list and pantry with
  Hana and 1 other." Primary "Join" (verified users) or the sign-in sheet; ghost "Not now" to `/`.
  Expired: the 19a error "This invite has expired" with "Ask Hana for a new link." After joining,
  go to `/kitchen` with the toast "You're in Hana's kitchen".

## Notes

- Built: `/join/:code` (`pages/JoinKitchenPage.tsx`, outside AppShell), the household header on Kitchen and the avatar pill on Pantry (`components/kitchen/KitchenPeople.tsx`: AvatarStack, PeopleSheet, InviteSheet), `sharedWithLine` in `lib/kitchen.ts`, the `join-kitchen` AuthAction and a `Users` icon.
- Invite reads are verified-only, so signed out the sheet is generic ("Join a kitchen"); the email link returns to `/join/:code` and the user taps Join. No pending action auto-joins (DECISIONS.md).
- The inviter's name comes from `profiles/{ownerUid}.displayName`, never the invite's `ownerName`. The body is "Share a shopping list and pantry with Hana." (no member count: a non-member cannot read the kitchen).
- Kitchen has no More menu: People (members, Remove, Leave) opens from the avatar stack (DECISIONS.md). Invite is owner only.
- Extra states: read failure (Try again), the owner's own link ("This is your own invite").
