after RL-022's deploy. Kept as a second deploy rather than folded into RL-022 so the P1
security rules are not held up by the terms text (RL-046) or the rate-limit work. Same
procedure and same reasoning as RL-022: the new client works under the old rules, the old
client does not work under the new ones (it writes no window documents and no terms acceptance),
so client first, rules last. The procedure for diffing the live ruleset is in
`docs/operations.md`.
