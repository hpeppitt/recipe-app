## Context

RL-166 supplies the ranking. Explore today (`src/pages/ExplorePage.tsx`, RL-098/099/126) has
the day-part header, the tile row, the chips row and the Recipes list. The design puts "Most
branched" between the tile row and the rest. Token mapping in this app: the spec's "surface" is
`bg-surface-secondary`, its ground is `bg-surface`; ramps are `accent-*`, `accent-2-*`,
`neutral-*`.

### The design (the implementer has no other copy)

- Section padding 22px 20px 0. Heading row: Caprasimo 18px "Most branched," followed by a
  32px-tall pill button (fully round, `bg-accent-200`, `text-accent-900`, Caprasimo 16px,
  padding 0 10px 0 12px, gap 4px) showing the range label ("all time", "last 30 days",
  "last 90 days") and a `ChevronDown`.
- The pill opens a small menu below it (min-width 170px, 6px padding, 22px radius, ground
  background, `shadow-lg`): rows 40px tall, 16px radius, 14px text, "All time", "Last 30
  days", "Last 90 days", the current one bold on `bg-accent-100` with a `Check`. Escape and an
  outside tap close it; arrow keys move between rows (a `role="menu"` or a listbox, pick one and
  log it).
- Three rows, each one button-link to the family's original, 8px vertical padding, gap 12px:
  a 36px rank circle (Caprasimo 16px; rank 1 `bg-accent` with `text-on-accent`, ranks 2 and 3
  `bg-surface-secondary`), a 48px round emoji circle on `bg-surface-secondary` (24px emoji),
  then the title (15px bold) over a meta line (12px `neutral-700`) holding the author
  `UserPill` and the total time, and at the right an `accent-2` tag with `GitBranch` and the
  count (prefixed "+" for the 30 and 90 day ranges).
- Default range: All time, or Last 90 days when RL-166 reports `allTimeAvailable: false` (then
  the All time row is not offered). The choice is not remembered across visits.
- Hidden while loading (no skeleton, so the list below does not jump: reserve nothing), when the
  ranking is empty, under a tag chip or the Following chip, and in local-only mode. A failure
  hides it silently (reported by the hook).
