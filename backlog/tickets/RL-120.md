## Context

RL-118 and RL-119 let a user attach up to three photos to a recipe chat message. They go to
Gemini through Firebase AI Logic on the free tier, are never stored, and are resent with each
later turn of the same chat (the SDK `ChatSession` keeps the history). Today
`src/content/legal/privacy.ts` only covers "what you type": the short version (line 26) and
the "AI recipe generation" section (lines 120 to 129). Photos of a fridge, a handwritten card
or a printed page can show people, addresses or handwriting, which that wording does not
cover.

**Author's decision (2026-10-19, /groom):** edit the Privacy Policy and add a one-line notice
in the composer (the notice is RL-119's, which now depends on this ticket). **No Terms change:**
do not touch `src/content/legal/terms.ts`, `TERMS_VERSION` or `firestore.rules`.

Why a privacy-only date bump is safe: `src/lib/legal.test.ts` checks that each document's
`lastUpdated` is a valid ISO date equal to its exported constant, and separately that the
`termsVersion()` literal in `firestore.rules` equals `TERMS_VERSION`, which is
`TERMS.lastUpdated` (`src/lib/legal.ts`). `PRIVACY_LAST_UPDATED` feeds no version and no
rules literal, so bumping it trips no test and asks no user to re-accept anything.

Approved under the "What needs a human" rule in `backlog/README.md`: the author approved this
change at /groom, and the exact wording still waits for the author's sign-off before
`/release` (the RL-046 rule). The voice to match is the existing policy's: short plain
sentences, "we", Australian spelling ("favourites", "organisation").

The downscale sentence below depends on `downscaleToJpeg` (RL-118,
`src/services/imageDownscale.ts`) redrawing the photo on a canvas and re-encoding it, which
drops the file's EXIF data, including GPS location. RL-118 specifies the same canvas path as
`AvatarEditor`. Check the merged code before keeping the sentence.
