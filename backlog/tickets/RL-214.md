## Context

The page only; RL-215 puts it in the nav. Inside `AppShell` like `/saved`. Counts come from
`useKitchen` (RL-213) and `useOwnCooks` (RL-196); the Saved row reuses the Saved page's list
(`useSavedFavorites`, `buildSavedList`, RL-091/121). The household avatars and "Shared with Ola
and Sami" line arrive in Phase 3 (RL-239). Token mapping: spec "surface" is
`bg-surface-secondary`, ground is `bg-surface`.

### The design (the implementer has no other copy)

- Header (52px top padding on mobile, 20px sides): Caprasimo 30px "Kitchen".
- Tiles (18px 20px 0): two-column grid, gap 12px. Each a link, padding 16px, 28px radius, min
  height 118px, gap 12px: a 40px circle on the ground holding a 20px icon, then Caprasimo 17px
  title over a 12px sub-line.
  - Shopping list: `bg-accent-200`, `text-accent-900`, icon `ShoppingBasket` in `accent-700`,
    sub "11 to buy" (or "Nothing to buy"). → `/kitchen/list`.
  - Pantry: `bg-accent-2-200`, `text-accent-2-900`, icon `Refrigerator` in `accent-2-700`, sub
    "23 things". → `/kitchen/pantry`.
  - This week: `bg-surface-secondary`, icon `CalendarDays` in `neutral-800`, sub "Soon". Not a
    link; `aria-disabled`.
  - Cook log: `bg-surface-secondary`, icon `ChefHat`, sub "14 cooks". → `/kitchen/log`.
- Saved (18px 20px 0): Caprasimo 18px "Saved" with a "See all" link (13px `accent-700`) to
  `/saved`; a sideways row of 84px tiles, 26px radius, the emoji at 40px on rotating tints
  (`accent-200`, `accent-2-200`, `bg-surface-secondary`), each a link to the recipe. Empty: one
  line "Tap the bookmark on any recipe and it'll wait for you here."
- Signed out or anonymous: tiles replaced by a 19a card "Sign in to keep a shopping list and
  pantry" with Sign in (the sheet with `AuthAction` `{ kind: 'continue' }`); the Saved row still
  shows device bookmarks.

## Notes

RL-213: useKitchen drops any action started while a kitchen write is in flight (up to 5 s), so disable the kitchen controls while a write runs, or a second tap is silently lost. kitchen is null until the first snapshot (show a skeleton, not an empty list).

Implemented: `KitchenPage` with `useKitchen`, one-shot `countUserCooks` for the cook count (not `useOwnCooks`), icons ShoppingBasket and CalendarDays added (Refrigerator existed). Unverified signed-in users get the sign-in card without a button (nothing to sign in to). Skeleton tiles also show while auth loads. `/kitchen/list` and `/kitchen/pantry` have no routes yet. The Saved row does not show a skeleton while loading, and offline/failed favourites notes from the Saved page are not repeated. Tiles are not disabled during writes: the page makes none.
