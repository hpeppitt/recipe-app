## Context

The author picked 18a ("Pills + inline stepper + expandable nutrition"). Spec
(`docs/design/organic/export/App Spec.dc.html`, Original recipe): "pills (author, time,
difficulty, kcal), ingredients with stepper (18a)", "Ingredients: Tap to tick off while
cooking (sage). Scaled amounts turn accent-700 with a "Scaled ×n" strip and Reset." Markup:
18a in `Recipe Parts and System.dc.html` and the pills, stepper, `scaled` and `rows` parts of
the `recipe` block in `App Prototype.dc.html`.

Today `RecipeContent` renders `MetadataPills` (emoji icons: time, difficulty, servings, prep,
cook), then `ServingStepper` ("Scale to", minus, count, plus, "Reset to N"), then
`NutritionPanel`, then `IngredientList` (native checkboxes when `checkable`, groups, unit
conversion through `useUnitSystem`), then `InstructionList`. Scaling is display only and must
stay so (`docs/decisions.md`, "Serving scaling is a display transform"): ingredient amounts
only, counts round to halves, the scale resets per recipe (the `key` on `RecipeContent`).
Ticks are not persisted. All of that stays. `scaleNote` comes from RL-087.

**Design token map:** `--color-bg` is `bg-surface`; `--color-surface` is
`bg-surface-secondary`; `tag-neutral` is `bg-neutral-100 text-neutral-800`; `tag-accent` is
`bg-accent-100 text-accent-800`. Light only: no `dark:` classes. 44px targets.

Values to copy:

- **Pills row**: flex wrap, gap 6px, margin-top 14px. Pills are 13px, padding `5px 12px`,
  gap 5px, `rounded-full`, Lucide icons instead of emoji: the author `UserPill` (RL-088)
  wrapped in a link to `/profile/<uid>` when there is a uid, labelled "Open author";
  `tag-neutral` `Clock` total time; `tag-neutral` `Gauge` difficulty; `tag-accent` 600
  `Flame` "<calories> kcal" only when `nutrition` exists. Prep and cook pills, when both are
  set, stay as two more `tag-neutral` pills with `Clock`. The servings pill moves into the
  stepper. On the chat card (`RecipeCardMessage`) the author pill is absent (no author yet)
  and servings stay as a pill.
- **Ingredients heading row**: margin-top 24px, flex, align centre. `h3` "Ingredients"
  (design h4 size, `font-heading` 20px), flex 1. Stepper on the right: a pill track
  `bg-surface-secondary`, padding 3px, gap 4px; two 34px `bg-surface` round buttons with
  `Minus` and `Plus` (44px hit area; existing labels "One fewer serving" and "One more
  serving", existing 1 to 99 bounds); between them the count, 13px 600, min-width 64px,
  centred, `aria-live="polite"`, "<n> serving(s)".
- **Scaled strip** (only when scaled): margin-top 10px, flex, gap 10px, padding `10px 14px`,
  `rounded-[18px]`, `bg-accent-100`, 13px `text-accent-900`, `Scale` icon, the
  `scaleNote` text, then a "Reset" button (underlined, 13px, inherits colour, 44px hit area)
  that restores the stored servings. It replaces "Reset to N".
- **Ingredient rows** (checkable): each row is the whole tick target, flex, gap 12px,
  padding `9px 0` (min height 44px), bottom rule 1px of the text colour at 8%
  (`border-text-primary/8`), 15px. A 22px circle with a 2px `border-neutral-400` and no fill
  when unticked; ticked, `bg-accent-2-600 border-accent-2-600` with a cream `Check` (12px).
  Then the amount (and unit) in 700 with `min-width: 62px`, `text-accent-700` when scaled;
  then the name (and notes in `text-text-tertiary`); ticked rows strike the name. Keep a
  real checkbox for semantics (a visually hidden native input inside the label, or
  `role="checkbox"` with `aria-checked` and Space), named by the ingredient text. Group
  headings stay, 14px 600 `text-text-secondary`.
- **Non-checkable list** (chat card): same row layout without the circle.

- [x] Every tick target is at least 44px tall and keyboard operable; the stepper keeps its labels and live count.

## Notes

Pills, stepper (with a separate `ScaledStrip` in the same file) and rows are restyled to 18a.
`IngredientList` gained `headingAside`, `belowHeading` and `scaled` props so the heading row
and strip live with the rows without changing the other callers (chat card, shared page).
`MetadataPills` takes optional `author`, `nutrition` and `servings`; the servings pill shows
only when `servings` is passed (chat card, compact preview, shared page). The author pill is
skipped for uid `local`. The stepper now needs ingredients to render (it lives in their
heading); a recipe with no ingredients array shows the missing-section notice instead.
