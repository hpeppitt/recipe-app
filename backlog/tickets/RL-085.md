## Context

After RL-078 a verified user's own recipes are a Dexie cache of Firestore. On a shared
device they stay after sign-out and show under the next account's Saved. Author answer of
2026-10-18: clear them on sign-out. The cloud copy is the truth (decision 1b,
`docs/decisions.md`), so nothing is lost: the listener refills the device on the next
sign-in.

Sign-out is `handleSignOut` in `src/contexts/AuthContext.tsx` (it clears the anonymous uid
and the stored Terms tick, then awaits `signOut()`). RL-078's listener stops on the uid
change and ignores a snapshot that arrives after it, so clearing after `signOut()` resolves
cannot race a late write. Keep the Dexie `get()`/`put()` rule in mind; this is a delete, so
`where`/`filter` plus `bulkDelete` is fine.

The only sign-out confirmation (`ProfilePage.tsx`, "Sign out of this anonymous account?")
is shown to anonymous accounts, which own no recipes (decision 1a), and its line "Recipes
on this device stay here" stays true for them. Verified sign-out has no confirmation and
Settings says nothing about sign-out, so no copy changes.

RL-076's tidy rule (rows of other uids that signed in on this device) stays. This ticket
covers shared devices going forward; the tidy still catches rows from before it ships,
including the pre-wipe ones.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `clearOwnRecipes(uid)` in `src/db/recipes.ts` deletes, in one `rw` transaction on `db.recipes`, every row with `createdBy.uid === uid`, and returns the count. Saved copies of other people's recipes, `'local'` rows and rows of other uids are untouched, and so is `db.favorites`.
- [ ] `handleSignOut` records whether the user was verified before signing out, and only for a verified user calls `clearOwnRecipes` with their uid after `signOut()` resolves. A failure is logged and reported as `sign-out-clear` and does not block sign-out.
- [ ] Anonymous and signed-out flows are unchanged, local bookmarks included. Nothing is written to Firestore.
- [ ] `src/db/recipes.test.ts` covers own rows removed, a saved copy, a `'local'` row, another uid's row and favourites kept.
- [ ] `docs/capabilities.md` (the sign-out behaviour, and the Library tabs row) and the 1b entry in `docs/decisions.md` say that a verified sign-out removes that account's recipes from the device and they reload on the next sign-in.
- [ ] A `backlog/QA.md` entry: on the preview, as a verified user with recipes and one saved copy of someone else's recipe, sign out; My Recipes keeps only the saved copy; sign back in and the account's recipes return. Repeat as an anonymous user with a bookmark and confirm it survives sign-out.
- [ ] `npm run build`, `npm run lint` and `npm test` pass.

## Out of scope

Clearing favourites, settings or other local storage on sign-out. Any new confirmation
dialog. Removing the RL-076 tidy rule.

## Verification

`npm test`. Human: the sign-out walk in the QA entry.

## Notes

Accepted trade: after a verified sign-out made offline, the account's recipes are not on the
device until it is online and signed in again.
