## Context

`migrateFavoritesUid` (`db/favorites.ts` lines 11-21) deletes and re-puts each favourite in
separate operations with no transaction, so a failure midway leaves some favourites under
the old uid and some lost. It is untested.

After the 2026-10-14 decision (RL-040) this is the main thing an anonymous account carries
into an email account: its local bookmarks. RL-032 removes the cloud half of the migration
and keeps this one, which is why it is worth making atomic. `src/db/recipes.test.ts` shows the
`fake-indexeddb` setup.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The read, deletes and puts run inside `db.transaction('rw', db.favorites, ...)`.
- [ ] `createdAt` is preserved, as now.
- [ ] `src/db/favorites.test.ts` covers: favourites move to the new uid, other users' favourites are untouched, and a put that throws mid-loop leaves every favourite under the old uid.

## Out of scope

The cloud half of the migration and any Dexie schema change.
