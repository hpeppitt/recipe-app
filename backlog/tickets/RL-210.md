## Context

Phase 2 adds per-user data the app has never stored: a shopping list, a pantry and
collections. Phase 3 (29a) makes the pantry, and the list, shared by a household; Phase 4 (27b)
publishes a collection as a cookbook. Constraints: decision 1b (a verified user's data lives in
the cloud and is the same on every device), decision 1a (anonymous accounts hold local
bookmarks only), a Dexie schema bump is irreversible (avoid one), favourites are owner-only
reads, and new collections need rules, export and erase coverage. The design decisions "24a
Kitchen replaces Saved" and "27a now, 27b later" are already taken in the spec; this ticket
confirms them with the storage. RL-211 to RL-227 are written for the recommendations.

## Questions and the groomer's recommendation

1. **Kitchen document**: one Firestore document `kitchens/{kitchenId}` per kitchen holding
   `memberUids: string[]`, `ownerUid`, `shopping: Item[]`, `pantry: PantryItem[]`, `updatedAt`.
   In Phase 2 a user's kitchen id is their uid and `memberUids` is `[uid]` (recommended: one
   listener, atomic updates, well under 1 MiB at the caps below, and Phase 3 only adds members).
   Item: `{ id, name, amount, unit, aisle, sources: { recipeId, emoji, title }[], done }`.
   PantryItem: `{ id, name, group: 'fresh' | 'cupboard', addedBy, addedAt }`.
2. **Caps**: 300 shopping items and 300 pantry items, enforced in the rules by list size
   (recommended).
3. **Collections**: `collections/{autoId}` with `ownerUid, name (<= 60), recipeIds (<= 200),
   createdAt, updatedAt, visibility: 'private'`, owner-only read and write in Phase 2
   (recommended; Phase 4 adds `public`).
4. **Who**: verified accounts only. Signed-out and anonymous users see the Kitchen tab with a
   sign-in prompt and their Saved row (device bookmarks) (recommended). Alternative: a
   device-only list in localStorage for guests.
5. **Nav**: Saved leaves the tab bar for Kitchen (`cooking-pot`); `/saved` stays as a page
   reached from Kitchen's Saved row; a one-time tip "Saved lives in Kitchen now" (recommended,
   as the spec decided).
6. **This week**: a stub tile with "Soon", no data (recommended; plan generation is not on the
   roadmap).
7. **Data rights and privacy**: kitchens and collections join Download my data and the erase
   script; a privacy paragraph is drafted in RL-226 (recommended).
