## Context

Each policy carries a `draftNotice` ("Draft. Not legal advice. The operator should have this
reviewed by a qualified lawyer before relying on it."), rendered by `LegalPage` as a
`role="note"` banner and pinned by `legal.test.ts` and `LegalPage.test.tsx`. The author approved
the wording on 2026-10-22 (RL-267) and wants the banner gone in v0.4.0.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `draftNotice` is removed from the `LegalDocument` type, the three content files and `LegalPage`; no draft banner renders on /terms, /privacy or /cookies.
- [ ] The tests that pinned the notice now assert that no draft banner (`role="note"` with the old text) renders. No other assertion is removed.
- [ ] The author-facing comments that call the files a draft for RL-046 sign-off say they were approved on 2026-10-22. `docs/legal/claims-to-verify.md` notes the banner removal.
- [ ] `backlog/QA.md` has a `### RL-268` section: open each policy page at 390px and check the banner is gone and the page still reads well.
- [ ] `npm run build`, `npm run lint` and `npm test` exit 0.

## Out of scope

Any wording change, `TERMS_VERSION` or `lastUpdated` (no text a user agrees to changes).
