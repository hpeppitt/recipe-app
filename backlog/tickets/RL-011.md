## Context

Recipes published anonymously before the verified-email gate are still in the shared
library, owned by accounts that can no longer publish. The rules deliberately keep owner
update and delete open for them. This is data hygiene, not a broken state, and it needs a
console count and a decision.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The count of such recipes is known.
- [ ] A decision (leave, or delete after a notice) is recorded in `docs/decisions.md`.

## Out of scope

Any client code.

## Verification

Human only.

## Notes

Superseded by RL-042 (2026-10-14 regroom). The author decided anonymous accounts are
local-bookmark only and authorised wiping all existing production data, so these recipes
are deleted by the wipe rather than decided on individually. Drop this ticket when RL-042 is
approved.
