## Context

29a makes the pantry (and so the shopping list, which lives in the same kitchen document per
RL-210) shared by two or three people, with each item showing who added it, and an invite that
"reuses 5a with Join Hana's kitchen". This is the first data in the app that one user writes and
another private user reads and changes, so it needs the author: who sees what changes. Rules can
check membership with `request.auth.uid in resource.data.memberUids` but cannot run a server
step, so joining must be provable from the request alone. RL-237 to RL-241 and RL-248 are
written for the recommendations.

## Questions and the groomer's recommendation

1. **One kitchen per person at a time**: a user belongs to exactly one kitchen; their own
   (id = uid) until they join another (recommended).
2. **Size**: at most 6 members (recommended).
3. **Invite**: the owner creates `kitchenInvites/{code}` (`code` 20 random characters,
   `{ kitchenId, ownerUid, ownerName, expiresAt (7 days), createdAt }`), shares a link
   `/join/<code>`. Anyone with a verified email and the link can join (recommended). An invite
   can be used several times until it expires or the owner deletes it.
4. **Joining**: the joiner updates the kitchen adding only their own uid to `memberUids`, allowed
   when `get(kitchenInvites/$(code))` exists, matches the kitchen and is unexpired; the code is
   passed as a field `joinCode` the rules check (recommended). Their own Phase 2 kitchen is kept
   but unused; nothing is merged (recommended; alternative: merge their pantry in).
5. **Leaving**: a member removes their own uid; the owner can remove anyone; the owner cannot
   leave while others remain (recommended).
6. **What members see**: everything in the kitchen (list, pantry, who added what and when),
   their names and avatars; not each other's cook logs, collections or taste profiles
   (recommended).
7. **Kitchen id discovery**: `kitchens where memberUids array-contains uid limit 1`, no composite
   index (recommended).
8. **Privacy policy**: a household paragraph in RL-248.
