## Context

Outside `AppShell`, own back button, like `/settings`. Data from `useTasteProfile` (RL-231).

### The design (the implementer has no other copy)

- Back row; Caprasimo 30px "Your taste"; 13px `neutral-700` headline "Learned from 14 cooks and 6
  branches. Tap anything to correct it."
- Three cards (gap 12px), `bg-surface-secondary`, 28px radius, padding 16px: a header with a 20px
  icon and the title 15px bold, and at the right a 12px `neutral-700` source.
  - "Always avoid" · `ShieldCheck` · "You told us": chips filled `bg-accent-2-700`
    `text-accent-2-100`, each with an `X`; plus a dashed "Add" chip that becomes a field.
  - "You cook a lot of" · `Flame` · "From 14 cooks": chips on the ground (`bg-surface`), `X`
    hides the term.
  - "You tend to change" · `GitBranch` · "From 6 branches": the same.
- Empty learned cards say "Nothing yet."
- Settings: a row "Your taste" with `Sparkles` and a chevron in the Account card, verified users
  only.
