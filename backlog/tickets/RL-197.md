## Context

The sheet component only; RL-198 opens it from the recipe page, RL-203 from cook mode. It uses
`components/ui/Sheet.tsx`. No stars anywhere: the two buttons are the vote. The photo tile is
RL-205 and the leftovers row is RL-221; leave a slot for neither. Token mapping: spec
"surface" is `bg-surface-secondary`, ground is `bg-surface`.

### The design (the implementer has no other copy)

Sheet padding 12px 22px 28px, 14px gaps:
- Caprasimo 26px, line-height 1.1: "You made it. How did it go?" (the sheet's label).
- Two buttons side by side, gap 8px, each flex 1, 50px tall, fully round, Caprasimo 14px, icon
  gap 6px: "Would make again" (`Repeat`; selected and default style `bg-accent-2-700`,
  `text-accent-2-100`) and "Needs a tweak" (`Wrench`; unselected style an inset 2px `divider`
  ring on the ground). They act as a radio group (`role="radiogroup"`, each `role="radio"`); the
  selected one takes the filled style. Nothing is selected at first.
- A note field: 96px min height, 24px radius, `bg-neutral-100`, 14px, placeholder "How did it
  go? What would you change?", 500 characters max with a counter from 450. Under it, 12px
  `neutral-700`: "Notes show on the recipe for other cooks." (copy from RL-192 answer 4).
- When the note has text and the vote is Needs a tweak: a row on `bg-accent-100`, 22px radius,
  padding 12px 14px, 13px `text-accent-900`, `GitBranchPlus` icon: "Turn the note into a
  branch: **"<noteToBranchPrompt>"**" (ellipsised to one line) and a 32px pill "Grow" on the
  ground. Grow saves the cook first, then opens `/recipe/<id>/vary` with
  `state: { suggestion: <prompt>, fromCookId: <id> }`.
- Primary 52px block "Save to my cook log", disabled until a vote is chosen. Saving shows
  "Saving…"; success closes the sheet and toasts "Saved to your cook log"; failure keeps the
  sheet with "Couldn't save that. Check your connection and try again." or the rate-limit copy.

## Notes
Added `Repeat` and `Wrench` icons to `ui/icons.tsx`. The toast is rendered by `CookedSheet` itself (3 s), so it needs no caller wiring; Grow navigates away so the toast is brief there. The sheet body mounts per open so state resets. Save is also disabled with no signed-in user. Focus starts on the first vote button via the dialog's first-focusable rule; not asserted in jsdom.
