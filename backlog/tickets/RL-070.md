## Context

RL-067's shell lists available views in one constant; this ticket appends Timeline, which
makes the Tree / Timeline switch appear. Layout comes from `layoutTimeline` (RL-069): it
returns stops with x, lane, line and stop number, and lines with label, colour token name
and branch point. Change tags come from `summariseChanges(diffRecipes(parent, stop), 3)`
(RL-066), where the parent is the stop's `parentId` record in the same family; the root has
no tags. Relative time is `timeAgo` in `src/lib/utils.ts`.

There is no bottom-sheet component. `ConfirmDialog` shows the house pattern for a native
`<dialog>` with `showModal`, labelled by its heading; follow it for the sheet.

Design 3b, to copy:

- Horizontal scrolling SVG. Lane spacing 44px; lines 6px wide with round caps and joins,
  coloured from the line's token (`var(--color-<token>)`); a line leaves its branch-point
  stop with a rounded bend into its lane. Stops are 14px circles: outline 3px in the line
  colour on `surface` fill; the current recipe's stop is filled terracotta (`accent`) with a
  2px accent ring at 3px offset.
- Each line's label sits at its start, 12px `text-text-secondary`; the trunk is "Original".
- Sheet (bottom, `rounded-card` top corners, `shadow-lg`, drag handle bar 36x4 px
  `neutral-400`): eyebrow `<line label> · stop <n>` (Organic h6: 13px uppercase, 0.08em
  tracking, `text-text-tertiary`), title in `font-heading` 25px, then
  `"<prompt>" · <author> · <relative time>` (prompt fallback "Variation", author fallback
  "Anonymous"; omit the time when `createdAt` is 0), then change tags as Organic tags
  (`+` and `-` amount tags accent: `bg-primary-50 text-primary-700`; `~` tags sage:
  `bg-accent-2-100 text-accent-2-800`, 9.12:1), then two buttons: secondary "Open"
  (`/recipe/:id`) and primary "Branch here" (`/recipe/:id/vary`).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `src/components/tree/TimelineView.tsx` renders the family per Context; `VersionTreePage` adds `timeline` to its view list, so `?view=timeline` shows it and the switch shows Tree and Timeline.
- [ ] Every stop is a focusable `<button>` (an SVG `<g role="button" tabindex="0">` or an HTML button overlaid on the stop; implementer's choice) with an accessible name `"<title>, <line label>, stop <n>"` and "current version" appended for the current one; Enter and Space open the sheet. Tab order follows x order.
- [ ] On mount the scroll container is scrolled so the current stop is in view (centred where possible); a family narrower than the viewport is not scrollable.
- [ ] The sheet is a `<dialog>` opened with `showModal`, labelled by its title, closed by Escape, a tap on the backdrop, or after Open or Branch here navigates. Focus returns to the stop that opened it.
- [ ] Readable from 1 to 30 versions: at 30 (a test fixture) labels do not overlap stops; 1 version renders a single stop and the label "Original".
- [ ] `src/components/tree/TimelineView.test.tsx` covers: stops render as buttons with the accessible names; keyboard opens the sheet; sheet shows line, stop number, title, prompt line, change tags from a real parent and child; root sheet shows no tags; Open and Branch here navigate to the right paths.
- [ ] `docs/capabilities.md` "Version tree view" row mentions the Timeline view.
- [ ] A `backlog/QA.md` entry: on a family of 1, about 8 and about 30 versions, open `?view=timeline` from a deep version and check it starts scrolled to the filled stop, swipe scroll is smooth on iOS Safari, tapping a stop opens the sheet with sensible change tags, Open and Branch here work, and a keyboard user can Tab through stops and open the sheet. At 390 and 320 px.
