## Context

1b's first section, "Branching fast this week", is a horizontal scroll-snap row of 200px
tiles (`docs/design/organic/export/App Spec.dc.html`; markup in the `explore` block of
`App Prototype.dc.html`). Ranking by this week's branches needs data the app does not have
(see Out of scope), so this ticket ships the same row filled with the newest recipes already
in the Explore feed's first page, titled "New this week" when every tile is from the last 7
days and "Newest" otherwise. When the ranking data exists, only the source and title change.

**Design token map:** `--color-surface` is `bg-surface-secondary`; neutral-700 text is
`text-text-tertiary`. Light only: no `dark:` classes. 44px targets.

Values to copy:

- Section: padding `20px 0 0 20px`. Heading row with `padding-right: 20px`,
  `margin-bottom: 10px`: the title in `font-heading` 18px.
- Row: flex, gap 12px, horizontal scroll, `padding-right: 20px; padding-bottom: 6px`,
  `scroll-snap-type: x mandatory`, scrollbar hidden.
- Tile (link to `/recipe/:id`, `scroll-snap-align: start`): 200px wide, column, gap 8px.
  A 200x200 square, `rounded-[30px]`, emoji centred at 76px, tint rotating by index
  `bg-accent-200`, `bg-accent-2-200`, `bg-surface-secondary`. When `childCount > 0`, a tag
  at `top: 12px; left: 12px`: `bg-accent-2-700 text-accent-2-100`, 600, 11px, `GitBranch`
  icon, "<n> branches" ("1 branch"). Under the square the title in `font-heading` 17px,
  line-height 1.15, padding `0 4px`; then a 12px `text-text-tertiary` row, gap 8px, padding
  `0 4px`: `UserPill` and `formatTime(totalTime)`.
- Up to 8 tiles: the first 8 roots of the Explore feed (before any search, chip or tag
  filter). The row hides while searching or filtering, when the feed is empty, in local-only
  mode, and while loading (the list skeleton covers loading).
- The row is a `region` named by its heading; tiles are reachable by Tab in order.
