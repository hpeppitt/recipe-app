## Context

RL-186 added a plain "Add from a link" row to `/new`. 30a replaces it with two tiles; the
sentence and Write it stay as they are.

### The design (the implementer has no other copy)

Under the sentence and above Write it, a two-column grid, gap 10px. Each tile a link, padding
16px, 28px radius, min height 110px, gap 10px: a 40px circle on the ground with the icon, then
Caprasimo 16px title over a 12px sub-line.
- "Paste a link" · "From any recipe site": `bg-accent-2-200`, `text-accent-2-900`, `Link` icon in
  `accent-2-700`. → `/import`.
- "Snap a page" · "Cookbook or handwritten card": `bg-accent-200`, `text-accent-900`, `Camera` in
  `accent-700`. → `/import?mode=photo` (RL-251).
