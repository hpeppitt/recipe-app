## Context

Explore today: "Branching fast this week" tiles (RL-126, `RecipeTileRow`) and "Most branched"
(RL-167, `MostBranchedList`). Data from RL-242. Branch counts stay, smaller, in the meta line.

### The design (the implementer has no other copy)

- **Cooked this week** (22px 0 0 20px): heading Caprasimo 18px with a "See all" link (13px
  semibold `accent-700`) to `/search`. 200px tiles, 30px radius, rotating tints, 76px emoji; a
  tag at the top left on the ground with `ChefHat` "12 cooked"; at the bottom left up to three
  22px avatars overlapping. Under each tile: Caprasimo 17px title and a 12px `neutral-700` meta
  "<author> · <GitBranch> 6".
- **Most cooked, [this month ▾]**: RL-167's component with the label pill offering "this month"
  (30 days) and "last 90 days", rows showing a sub-line "<author> · most cook the "<branch
  prompt>" branch" when the leader differs from the original, and the count at the right with
  `ChefHat`.
- When there are no cooks in the last 7 days, the tiles fall back to Branching fast this week;
  when none in the range, the ranked section falls back to Most branched.
