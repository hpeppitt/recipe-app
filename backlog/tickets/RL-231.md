## Notes

- From RL-230: the rules check each avoid (a string of at most `TASTE_TERM_MAX`, 40, at most
  `TASTE_AVOID_MAX`, 12) but bound `hidden` by total length only (`join('')` at most 50 x 40)
  and accept non-string hidden elements. The client must keep `hidden` to at most
  `TASTE_HIDDEN_MAX` (50) strings of at most 40 characters each (truncate or refuse a longer
  learned term before hiding it). Constants are in `src/lib/taste.ts`.
- Export and erase: wire tasteProfiles like notificationPrefs at every site (fetchCloudDataForExport exportSection and the unverified error-section map in src/services/firestore.ts; all hardcoded key arrays in scripts/data-request.mjs), each with a test. On read, drop non-string or over-length hidden entries. Normalise avoids to one line (collapse whitespace, strip control characters) before saving or sending.
- Hook is `useTasteProfile(cooks)`: the caller passes its own `useOwnCooks().cooks`, so no second listener or read opens. Cooked recipes' titles and tags come from Dexie by `recipeId`; a cook of a recipe not held locally is skipped (Cook carries no title or tags). Own branches are Dexie rows with `createdBy.uid == uid` and a parent.
- `saveTasteProfile` is a read-modify-write transaction writing all four keys (the rules need `hasAll` on create and update), not a merge; a timed-out write may still commit. The stored document is read once on mount; mutators throw if that read failed so a default document never overwrites the real one.
- Normalisers `normaliseAvoids` and `normaliseHidden` are in `src/lib/taste.ts`. A hidden term over 40 characters is dropped, not cut (a cut term would not match what `learnTaste` derives); at 50 the oldest go. An avoid beyond 12 is dropped silently; the UI ticket should show the cap.
- Export key is `cloud.tasteProfile` (verified only), erase and export in the script use `tasteProfile` from `tasteProfiles/{uid}`.
