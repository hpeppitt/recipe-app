## Context

Uses RL-276's slots. Depends on RL-275 only because both rewrite `CreatePage.tsx` and the
layout lands first.

### The design (the implementer has no other copy)

- A row under the sentence h1, `mt-5 flex flex-wrap gap-2`, of three `Chip`-style pill
  buttons (the existing `components/ui/Chip`, 44px hit area): "+ Another ingredient",
  "+ Add a time", "+ Add a method".
- Tapping one adds its word to the sentence as an underlined cycling word, styled exactly like
  the existing three (`text-accent-700`, 3px underline), and the chip becomes pressed
  (`aria-pressed="true"`) with its label changed to "Remove ingredient", "Remove time",
  "Remove method". Tapping it again removes the word.
- The added word cycles on tap like the others; the status line announces the new word, and
  "added" or "removed" when a chip is toggled.
- Write it sends `buildCreatePrompt` with the new slots.
