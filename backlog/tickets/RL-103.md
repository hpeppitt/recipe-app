## Context

Spec order for the recipe page (`docs/design/organic/export/App Spec.dc.html`, Original
recipe): "ingredients with stepper (18a), method, nutrition (collapsed), sibling carousel".
18a: "Nutrition is collapsed by default." Markup: 18a in `Recipe Parts and System.dc.html`
(the four-tile grid) and the `steps` and nutrition parts of the `recipe` block in
`App Prototype.dc.html`.

Today `NutritionPanel` sits above the ingredients in `RecipeContent`, always open, with four
cells (kcal, protein, carbs, fat), "estimated, per serving (of N)", and renders nothing
without data. Nutrition is per serving and does not scale (`docs/decisions.md`).
`InstructionList` renders numbered steps with optional ticks (`checkable`), and temperature
conversion; keep both.

**Design token map:** `--color-bg` is `bg-surface`; `--color-surface` is
`bg-surface-secondary`; neutral-700 text is `text-text-tertiary`. Light only: no `dark:`
classes. 44px targets.

Values to copy:

- **Method**: `h3` "Method" (`font-heading` 20px), margin `26px 0 8px`. Each step: flex,
  gap 12px, padding `8px 0`, 15px, line-height 1.5; a 30px `rounded-full`
  `bg-surface-secondary` disc with the step number in `font-heading` 14px. A ticked step (if
  `checkable` ticks exist today) strikes its text and fills the disc `bg-accent-2-600` with
  cream text; the tick target stays 44px.
- **Nutrition card**: below the method (before notes and tags), margin-top 18px,
  `rounded-[28px] bg-surface-secondary`, padding `14px 16px`, column, gap 12px. A header
  button (full width, flex, `aria-expanded`, 44px): "Nutrition per serving" in
  `font-heading` 17px, flex 1, then `ChevronDown` (closed) or `ChevronUp` (open). Closed by
  default, not remembered. Open: a 4-column grid, gap 8px, tiles padding `10px 6px`,
  `rounded-[18px] bg-surface`, centred, value `font-heading` 18px, label 11px
  `text-text-tertiary`: Calories (kcal, no unit), Carbs "<n> g", Fat "<n> g", Protein
  "<n> g", rounded as today. Under the grid, 11px `text-text-tertiary`: "Estimated from
  ingredients. Changes with each branch."
- No card at all when `nutrition` is missing (today's rule).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `InstructionList` and `NutritionPanel` render as above; `RecipeContent` orders the body: pills, ingredients, method, nutrition, notes, tags.
- [ ] Nutrition is collapsed on load and on each new recipe; the header button toggles it and exposes `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls`.
- [ ] The figures do not change with the servings stepper (per serving, as today).
- [ ] `NutritionPanel.test.tsx` (new) covers: nothing rendered without data, collapsed by default, open shows four tiles with units and the note. `RecipeContent.test.tsx` passes with the new order.
- [ ] The chat card (`RecipeCardMessage`) shows the same collapsed card.
- [ ] A `backlog/QA.md` entry: on a recipe with nutrition, open and close the card by tap and keyboard; scale servings and confirm the numbers stay; a recipe without nutrition shows no card; step discs at 320px with a long step.
- [ ] `npm test`, `npm run lint`, `npm run build` pass.

## Out of scope

The prototype's "Per serving / Total" switch and the 2,000 kcal bars (a total mode multiplies
estimates, which the scaling decision avoids; author question). Diet pills.
