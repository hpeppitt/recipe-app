## Context

There is no plan or "saved for tonight" in the app (This week is a stub, RL-214), so the only
trigger built is "opened in the last hour" (groomer choice, log it). The last opened recipe is
kept locally (`services/storage.ts`, `recipe-lab-last-opened` = `{ id, at }`), written by the
recipe page. Pantry overlap uses `itemKey` (RL-212) against `useKitchen` when available.

### The design (the implementer has no other copy)

Under the Explore header, 20px sides: a card on `bg-accent-200`, 28px radius, padding 14px,
gap 12px: a 56px circle on the ground with the emoji at 28px; then 12px bold `accent-800`
"TONIGHT · you opened this", Caprasimo 18px title, 12px `accent-900` "15 min · 3 of 6 things in
your pantry" (the pantry part only with a kitchen); a 40px pill "Cook" (`bg-accent`, Caprasimo
13px, `ChefHat`) to `/recipe/<id>/cook`. The card links to the recipe; a 32px close hides it
until a recipe is opened again.
