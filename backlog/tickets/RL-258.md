## Context

Public page, signed out works (reads are public). Reuses the 12b shell's slim banner from
`SharedRecipePage` (RL-113) and the 4c tiles (`RecipeTileGrid`). Recipes are read with
`getPublishedRecipe` (one read each, at most 200, fetched as the grid scrolls in pages of 20).
Following needs a verified email; others get the sign-in sheet with `{ kind: 'follow-cookbook' }`
("Sign in to follow this cookbook").

### The design (the implementer has no other copy)

- 12px bold `accent-700` kicker "COOKBOOK · by Hana" (the name links to the profile), Caprasimo
  30px name, 15px `neutral-800` description.
- Primary block "Follow this cookbook" (`BookOpen`), "Following" when on (secondary), with the
  follower count in 13px `neutral-700` under it.
- Two-column cards, gap 10px, 28px radius, `bg-surface-secondary`, padding 14px: the emoji at
  40px on a tint, title 15px bold, and 12px `neutral-700` "Less sugar branch" (the recipe's
  prompt as "<prompt> branch" for a branch) or "Original". Tap opens the recipe.
- Private or missing: the 19a error "This cookbook is private" / "This cookbook doesn't exist".
