## Context

`markAllNotificationsRead` (`services/firestore.ts` around line 568) puts every unread
notification in one `writeBatch`. Firestore batches are capped at 500 writes, so with more
than 500 unread the commit throws and nothing is marked. `migrateFirestoreUid` already shows
the chunking loop to copy.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Updates are committed in batches of at most 500.
- [ ] A test in the mocked-Firestore style of `src/services/firestore.favorites.test.ts` asserts that 501 unread produce two commits of 500 and 1, and 0 unread produce no commit.

## Out of scope

Changing the notification query or the 50-item subscription limit.

## Verification

`npm test`.
