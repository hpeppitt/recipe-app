## Context

The author picked 5a: "Contextual sheet ... Trigger First save, branch, suggest or follow.
Headline names the action. After sign-in, the pending action runs automatically. Guest Text
link with the trade-off stated." (`docs/design/organic/export/App Spec.dc.html`); 5b and 5c
for email. Markup: 5a, 5b, 5c in `Social Auth and Generate.dc.html` and the `sh.auth`,
`email`, `inbox` blocks in `App Prototype.dc.html`.

What exists and stays: `AuthModal` (choose, email, sent steps; anonymous sign-in; magic link
via `sendEmailLink`; the Terms box from RL-047, stored before the send and cleared on
failure), used when a signed-out visitor taps the heart and from the signed-out profile.
`SignUpDialog` wraps `EmailLinkingForm tone="invite"` for actions that need a verified email
(Share on an unpublished recipe, Follow, profile editing); `EmailLinkingForm` picks
`linkEmail` for anonymous and `sendEmailLink` for signed out. Anonymous accounts may only keep
local bookmarks (decision 1a), so the guest option is offered only where it works (the heart),
which is what `AuthModal` already does.

Not built here (new features, see Out of scope): Apple and Google sign-in, password sign-in
and its segmented control, the pending action running after sign-in, "Open mail app", the
resend timer. The magic link returns to the app's origin (`services/firebase.ts`,
`actionCodeSettings.url`), not to the page it was asked from, so the inbox copy must not
promise "you'll land back on your branch".

**Design token map:** `--color-bg` is `bg-surface`; `--color-surface` is
`bg-surface-secondary`; neutral-800 text is `text-text-secondary`; neutral-700 is
`text-text-tertiary`; filled terracotta with cream is `bg-primary-600 text-on-accent`. Light
only. 44px targets.

Values to copy, all inside `Sheet` (RL-089), gap 12px:

- **Choose (5a)**: a 56px circle `bg-accent-2-200 text-accent-2-800` with a 26px icon for the
  action (`Heart` for saving a favourite, `UserPlus` for follow, `Link` for share,
  `MessageCircle` for suggest, `GitBranchPlus` for branch, `UserRound` default),
  margin-top 6px. Headline `font-heading` 26px, line-height 1.1: "Sign in to <action>",
  where callers pass the action ("save this recipe", "follow <name>", "share this recipe",
  "edit your profile"); default "Sign in to continue". Body 14px `text-text-secondary`: the
  existing reason copy for that caller. Primary 52px full width, `Mail` icon, "Continue with
  email". Guest (AuthModal only): a ghost text button "Continue without an account" and,
  under it, 12px `text-text-tertiary` centred, max-width 290px: "Bookmarks stay on this
  device only. You can add an email later."
- **Email (5b)**: heading `font-heading` 26px "Continue with email"; the Terms box (unchanged
  component and rules); an email field (label "Email", pill 52px, 16px, 1.5px accent border
  on focus); primary 54px "Send me a link"; 13px `text-text-tertiary` "We'll email you a link.
  No password to remember."; a sage note `rounded-[22px] bg-accent-2-100 text-accent-2-900`,
  13px, padding `14px 16px`, `ShieldCheck` icon: "New here? The link also confirms your
  email, which you need before you can create, branch or suggest."; a back ghost button to
  the choose step.
- **Sent (5c)**: decorative circles (280px `bg-accent-200` top right off-canvas, 90px
  `bg-accent-2-300`), a 36px `MailOpen` in `text-accent`, heading `font-heading` 34px,
  line-height 1.05, "Check your inbox", body 15px: "We sent a sign-in link to <email as a
  `tag-neutral` 14px 600 pill>. Open it on this device and it brings you back to Recipe
  Lab."; a ghost "Use a different email" back to the email step; a secondary "Close".
- `SignUpDialog` and the `invite` tone of `EmailLinkingForm` use the same sheet, headline,
  icon and email step look (no guest link). The `warning` tone (profile banner) is restyled
  by RL-095, not here.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `AuthModal` and `SignUpDialog` render in `Sheet` with the steps above and take an `action` prop for the headline and icon; every caller passes its action. Behaviour, including anonymous sign-in, the Terms box storage and clearing, `linkEmail` versus `sendEmailLink`, and auto-dismiss when a user appears, is unchanged.
- [ ] No Apple, Google or password control exists; nothing claims the link returns to the current page.
- [ ] `TermsAcceptance.test.tsx` and any auth tests pass unchanged in assertions (selectors may change); add tests that the headline names the action and the guest link exists only in `AuthModal`.
- [ ] A `backlog/QA.md` entry: on the preview, signed out: tap the heart (guest link present), Follow (no guest link), Share on an unpublished recipe; send a link with and without the Terms box; Use a different email; open the link on the same device and on another device. Check 320px and VoiceOver reads the sheet title.
- [ ] `npm test`, `npm run lint`, `npm run build` pass.

## Out of scope

Apple, Google or password sign-in; remembering a link or password choice; running the pending
action after sign-in; the invite gate (5d); the email-link outcome states (RL-109); the
anonymous profile card (RL-095).
