## Context

Today Delete removes the recipe and its whole subtree (capabilities "Delete a recipe";
`deleteRecipeTree`, `deletePublishedRecipeTree`; decisions.md "Recipe delete waits for the
cloud and reports what remains (RL-023)"). Versions by other cooks are skipped because the rules
deny touching them ("removing those needs a Cloud Function"), so today they are left in the
cloud pointing at a parent that no longer exists. The spec wants "Delete version": the version
goes, its branches stay and move up to its parent. The author put this in v1 as design-first on
2026-10-20. The obstacle: a child written by someone else can only be re-pointed by its owner
(`isOwnerUpdate` in `firestore.rules`), and Blaze (Cloud Functions) is only for abuse
(decision 2). This ticket is the author's choice; RL-158 to RL-160 build it and are written for
the recommended answers below. It becomes `needs-human` when approved.

## Questions and the groomer's recommendation

1. **A version with branches** (not the original): move its direct children up to its parent
   (recommended), or keep today's cascade.
2. **Children by other cooks**: (a) a narrow new rule lets the deleted version's owner change
   only `parentId` and `depth` of its direct children, only in the same batch that deletes it
   (`existsAfter` false), only to the deleted version's own parent, depth minus one
   (recommended; no bill, emulator-testable); (b) a Cloud Function (needs Blaze, against
   decision 2); (c) only your own children move and a version with other cooks' children
   cannot be deleted.
3. **The original (root) with branches**: (a) as today, delete your own subtree and leave other
   cooks' versions orphaned; (b) refuse while other cooks have versions in the family, saying
   so in the menu (recommended); (c) promote the oldest child to original (rewrites `rootId`
   across the family, which needs every owner or a Cloud Function).
4. **Stored `depth`** after a move: update the moved children only and stop reading `depth` for
   anything but "is this a branch" (recommended; RL-128 derives the generation from the chain),
   or rewrite every descendant (impossible for other cooks' rows without (2b)).
5. **Telling the moved branches' authors**: no in v1 (recommended), or a notification.
6. **Grandchildren stay where they are** (under the moved children). Confirm.
