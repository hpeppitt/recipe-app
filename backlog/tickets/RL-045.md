## Context

RL-044 produces the policy content as structured TypeScript. This ticket renders it. Routes
live in `src/App.tsx` inside the `AppShell` layout; `SettingsPage.tsx` groups controls in
`<section>` blocks with uppercase `h2` headings (Appearance, Units, Data).

Releasing these pages changes what the app says about privacy, so the release that carries
them waits for the author's sign-off in RL-046.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Routes `/terms`, `/privacy` and `/cookies` render one shared `LegalPage` component from the RL-044 content, with a `TopBar` with back, the title, the last-updated date, and the sections as `h2` plus paragraphs, readable at 390 px in both themes.
- [x] The pages work signed out and in local-only mode (no Firebase calls).
- [x] `SettingsPage` gains a "Legal" section after "Data" with three links: Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy.
- [x] The email step of `AuthModal` and `EmailLinkingForm` show a small line linking to Terms and Privacy. The required checkbox is RL-047, not this ticket.
- [x] `docs/capabilities.md` gains a row for the legal pages.
- [x] A `backlog/QA.md` entry: open each page from Settings at 390 px in light and dark, signed out.

## Out of scope

The checkbox and acceptance record (RL-047), and editing the policy text.

## Verification

`npm run build`, `npm run lint`, then the browser check above.

## Notes

- Routes sit outside `AppShell` (like detail pages) so each page has its own `main` and back button. `PWA navigateFallbackDenylist` only blocks `/__/`, `/api/` and `/_*`, so `/terms`, `/privacy`, `/cookies` get the SPA fallback; Hosting rewrites `**` to index.html.
- Wording is "Read our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy." Nothing records agreement until RL-047, so "By continuing you agree" would be untrue today. RL-047 should change it when the checkbox lands.
- Links open in a new tab (`target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"`) so a half-finished sign-up is not lost. Not verified in an installed PWA: from reading, a same-scope link may open in the app window or an in-app browser; browser check in QA.
- Scroll-to-top on doc change is done; focus is not moved (the h1 is in TopBar). Tick for "criteria" 1 to 6 reflects code and tests; visual 390 px checks need a browser.
- Shared `LegalLinks` component serves AuthModal and EmailLinkingForm (so SignUpDialog and the chat gate too). No existing test changed.
