## Context

RL-044 produces the policy content as structured TypeScript. This ticket renders it. Routes
live in `src/App.tsx` inside the `AppShell` layout; `SettingsPage.tsx` groups controls in
`<section>` blocks with uppercase `h2` headings (Appearance, Units, Data).

Releasing these pages changes what the app says about privacy, so the release that carries
them waits for the author's sign-off in RL-046.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Routes `/terms`, `/privacy` and `/cookies` render one shared `LegalPage` component from the RL-044 content, with a `TopBar` with back, the title, the last-updated date, and the sections as `h2` plus paragraphs, readable at 390 px in both themes.
- [ ] The pages work signed out and in local-only mode (no Firebase calls).
- [ ] `SettingsPage` gains a "Legal" section after "Data" with three links: Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy.
- [ ] The email step of `AuthModal` and `EmailLinkingForm` show a small line linking to Terms and Privacy. The required checkbox is RL-047, not this ticket.
- [ ] `docs/capabilities.md` gains a row for the legal pages.
- [ ] A `backlog/QA.md` entry: open each page from Settings at 390 px in light and dark, signed out.

## Out of scope

The checkbox and acceptance record (RL-047), and editing the policy text.

## Verification

`npm run build`, `npm run lint`, then the browser check above.
