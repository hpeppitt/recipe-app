## Context

`/new` (`CreatePage.tsx`, Organic 6c, RL-125) shows the sentence first and the two import
tiles (RL-250) under it. The author wants the import tiles at the top, an "or", then the
sentence. The author also wants the green "What have you got?" card (Explore's link to
`/search?sheet=pantry`, which opens the RL-169 `PantrySheet`) on Create, at the bottom or next
to the import tiles.

Placement chosen: at the top, full width under the two tiles. The top block then reads as
"start from something you have" (a link, a page, your fridge) and the sentence as "start from
an idea". The bottom of the page is the sticky Write it / Describe it my own way bar; a card
there would compete with the primary button and sit under the bar on short screens. Log this
in `backlog/DECISIONS.md` so QA can overrule it.

### The design (the implementer has no other copy)

- Order, top to bottom: the decorative `accent-2-200` circle (unchanged), the two import tiles
  (unchanged markup, `grid-cols-2 gap-2.5`, `pt-6`), the pantry card (`mt-2.5`), the "or"
  divider, the hint "Tap the underlined words" (13px `text-text-tertiary`), the sentence h1
  (36px Caprasimo, `mt-3` instead of `mt-[30px]`), then the spacer and the sticky actions.
- Pantry card: extract Explore's card into `components/recipe/PantryCard.tsx` and use it in
  both places, unchanged: `rounded-[30px] bg-accent-2-200 p-[18px]`, 48px white circle with
  `Refrigerator`, "What have you got?" (Caprasimo 18px) over "Find or write a recipe from your
  fridge." (13px), `ChevronRight`, a `Link` to `/search?sheet=pantry`.
- "or" divider: a row `my-6 flex items-center gap-3`, two `h-px flex-1 bg-neutral-200` rules
  either side of "or" in 13px `text-text-tertiary`; the rules are `aria-hidden`.
- Local-only mode (`!isConfigured`): the import tiles stay hidden (RL-250); the pantry card
  shows (Search works on the device, as on Explore); the divider shows because the card is
  above it.
