## Context

The audit found about 110 dead lines. The 2026-10-14 decision (anonymous accounts are
local-bookmark only, RL-040) plus the wipe (RL-042) make more of the uid migration dead: an
anonymous account can no longer own any cloud data, so when it upgrades to an email account
that already exists there is nothing in Firestore to move and no recipe can be stranded.

What is now dead, each checked by grepping the whole tree for callers:

- `migrateFirestoreUid` (`services/firestore.ts`) in full. Its later steps were always
  denied by rules (see `docs/data-model.md` "Known rules limitation"); its recipes step now
  has nothing to move.
- The stranded-identity notice: `StrandedIdentity` and `describeStrandedIdentity` /
  `shouldNotify` in `lib/migration.ts` and their tests, `StrandedIdentityNotice.tsx` and its
  mount in `AppShell.tsx`, the get, set and clear functions in `services/storage.ts`, and the
  `setStrandedIdentity` call in `runMigration` (`AuthContext.tsx`).
- The `recipeCount` increment in `publishRecipe` (lines 59-66). Nothing reads `recipeCount`.
- `getRecipeChildren` and `getRecipeAncestors` (`db/recipes.ts` lines 45-63).
- `formatDate` (`lib/utils.ts` lines 12-18).
- The `previousUid` ternary on `services/firebase.ts` line 307: always `null` there.

What stays: the local carry-over. `runMigration` keeps calling `migrateRecipesUid` and
`migrateFavoritesUid` (Dexie) so local recipes and bookmarks follow the user into an existing
email account. `previousUid` from `completeEmailSignIn` still drives that.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `migrateFirestoreUid` and `UidMigrationOutcome` are deleted; `runMigration` runs only the two Dexie migrations.
- [ ] The stranded-identity notice and everything listed above that only serves it are deleted, including `src/lib/migration.test.ts` cases for deleted functions only. If `lib/migration.ts` ends up empty it is deleted.
- [ ] The `recipeCount` increment is removed. In `firestore.publish.test.ts` the test "bumps the creator profile recipeCount" is replaced by one asserting a first publish by a real uid calls no `updateDoc`; the "local placeholder" and "does not inflate on a retry" tests stay and still pass. The `recipeCount` field on `UserProfile` and the default in `createOrUpdateProfile` stay.
- [ ] `getRecipeChildren`, `getRecipeAncestors` and `formatDate` are deleted.
- [ ] Line 307's ternary becomes `previousUid: null`.
- [ ] `docs/data-model.md` "Known rules limitation" is removed or replaced by one paragraph on the local-only carry-over; `docs/decisions.md` product decision 2's sentence about roadmap 1.3's notice being the permanent answer gets a dated note that the notice was removed because nothing can be stranded; `docs/capabilities.md` "Anonymous → email upgrade" row matches.
- [ ] `npm run build`, `npm run lint` and `npm test` pass, with no test removed other than tests of deleted code.

## Out of scope

The Dexie migrations themselves (RL-034 makes the favourites one atomic), and deleting
`recipeCount` from documents (RL-042 wipes them anyway).

## Verification

The three gates. Human QA in `backlog/QA.md`: on the emulator or preview, bookmark as
anonymous, then sign in with an email that already has an account, and confirm the bookmark
and any local recipes carried over with no notice shown.
