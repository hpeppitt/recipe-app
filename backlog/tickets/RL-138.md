## Context

`TimelineView.tsx` draws each line's label beside its first stop (~43-55). When the view is
scrolled sideways (it centres the current stop on open), the labels of lanes that start off
screen are not visible, and colours mean nothing unprompted. Punch list: "First open: lane names
pinned at the left edge for 2 s, then collapse to dots." The author kept the time-scaled x axis
and 44px lane spacing (SPEC Overrides); do not touch the layout. "First open" is read as each
time the Timeline view mounts (not once per device); log that in `backlog/DECISIONS.md`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] An overlay pinned to the left edge of the scroller (`sticky left-0`, inside the scrolling element, `pointer-events-none`, `aria-hidden="true"`; the existing labels and stop names stay the accessible ones). One entry per lane at that lane's y (`laneY`): a 10px dot in the lane colour and the lane's label, 12px semibold `text-text-primary` on a `bg-surface/90` pill, max 160px with ellipsis.
- [x] On mount the names show; after 2000 ms the labels collapse and only the dots remain, 8px from the left edge. With motion allowed the collapse is a 250ms width and opacity transition (`motion-safe:`); under `prefers-reduced-motion` it switches with no transition.
- [x] The timer is cleared on unmount; switching away from Timeline and back shows the names again.
- [x] Tests with fake timers: names visible at mount, gone (dots present) after 2000 ms, timer cleared on unmount.
- [x] A `backlog/DECISIONS.md` row (every mount, not once per device).
- [x] A `backlog/QA.md` entry. `npm test`, `npm run lint`, `npm run build` pass.

## Out of scope

Timeline spacing, lane colours, stop sheet, x axis (kept by the author).

## Verification

Gates: tests, `ui-reviewer`. Human on a family with 4+ lanes at 390px: open Timeline, names
show for 2 s then become dots; scroll sideways, the dots stay at the left. Reduced motion on:
same, without animation.

## Notes
Legend overlay is a zero-size sticky anchor inside the scroller. Shows on every mount (logged in DECISIONS). Labels collapse via max-width and opacity (motion-safe 250ms), kept in the DOM with data-collapsed.
