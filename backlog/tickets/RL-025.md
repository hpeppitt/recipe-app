## Context

Two display bugs in the same path.

- `scaleAmount` (`lib/scale.ts` lines 41-47) snaps anything from 0.125 to about 0.19 up to
  0.25 because `KITCHEN_FRACTIONS` has no eighth, though its own comment calls an eighth the
  smallest spoon. Half a teaspoon for 4 scaled to 1 shows a quarter teaspoon, double the
  right amount.
- `formatAmount` (`IngredientList.tsx` lines 120-130) renders a non-matching fraction by
  slicing the decimal, giving ".44 cup" and ".8". It is untested. It rounds the fractional
  part to two decimals, so an eighth arrives as `0.13`, not `0.125`.

The scaling rules are recorded in "Serving scaling is a display transform" in
`docs/decisions.md`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] `KITCHEN_FRACTIONS` includes `0.125`; the comment listing drawable fractions includes ⅛.
- [x] `formatAmount` moves to `src/lib/amount.ts` (no imports from `services/` or `hooks/`), and `IngredientList` imports it.
- [x] It renders ⅛ for an eighth, including after its two-decimal rounding.
- [x] A fractional part with no glyph renders with a leading zero when there is no whole part ("0.44") and as a normal decimal otherwise ("1.44").
- [x] `src/lib/scale.test.ts` adds cases for raw 0.125 and 0.15 (both 0.125) and 0.19 (0.25).
- [x] `src/lib/amount.test.ts` covers 0.125, 0.25, 0.33, 0.44, 0.8, 1.5, 2 and 2.125.
- [x] `docs/decisions.md`, scaling entry, mentions the eighth.

## Out of scope

Unit pluralisation (RL-035), conversion rounding, and any change to count rounding.

## Verification

`npm test`. Human QA: on a recipe with "½ tsp" and 4 servings, scale to 1 and confirm "⅛ tsp".

## Notes

- Snapping is nearest-fraction with no tolerance; the midpoint of an eighth and a quarter is 0.1875. Below 0.125 the existing raw two-decimal arithmetic is unchanged.
- Glyph matching is by exact rounded key as before; 0.13 and 0.12 both map to the eighth. Behaviour for halves, thirds, quarters and mixed numbers is unchanged.
