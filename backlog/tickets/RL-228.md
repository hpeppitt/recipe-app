## Context

28b learns a taste profile from cooks and branches ("Learned from 14 cooks and 6 branches. Tap
anything to correct it."), shows it at `/settings/taste`, asks avoids inline the first time a
recipe is generated, and shows bold profile terms in the Create sentence. Avoids such as
peanuts or shellfish are likely allergies, which is health data under GDPR, and today prompts
carry no personal data. Prompts are built on the client (`lib/prompts.ts`) and sent to Gemini on
the free tier (decisions.md "Policy sign-off": testers are invited from outside the EEA, UK and
Switzerland while the free tier is used). So storage and sending both need the author.
RL-229 to RL-235 and RL-248 are written for the recommendations.

## Questions and the groomer's recommendation

1. **Learned parts are derived, not stored**: "You cook a lot of" (top tags and dish words of
   recipes the user cooked) and "You tend to change" (frequent phrases in the user's branch
   prompts) are computed on the device from the cook log and own branches each visit
   (recommended; nothing new to store or erase).
2. **Stored parts**: a private document `tasteProfiles/{uid}`, owner-only read and write,
   `{ avoids: string[] (<= 12, each <= 40), hidden: string[] (learned terms the user removed),
   askedAt: number | null, updatedAt }` (recommended). Not on the public profile.
3. **Sent to Gemini**: avoids only, as one line in the system prompt ("Never use: peanuts,
   pork."), for new recipes and branches; learned tastes are used only for the Create sentence's
   defaults, never sent (recommended). Alternative: send learned tastes too.
4. **Inline ask**: the first time a verified user generates, a card above the composer asks
   "Anything the kitchen should avoid?" with chips Peanuts, Tree nuts, Dairy, Gluten, Shellfish,
   Pork, Alcohol, Very spicy and "Something else"; Skip records `askedAt` and never asks again
   (recommended).
5. **Wording**: the card says avoids are a preference, not a medical guarantee: "We'll ask the
   AI to leave these out. Always check the ingredients if it's an allergy." (recommended).
6. **Data rights and privacy**: the document joins export and erase; the privacy policy names it
   as possibly health data, kept private, sent to Gemini as part of the prompt (RL-248).
7. **28a questionnaire**: not built (recommended, as the spec decided).
