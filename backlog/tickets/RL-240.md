## Context

RL-220 stores `addedBy` and `addedAt` per item. Ageing is approximate (the added date), used
only to nudge. Works for one-person kitchens too.

### The design (the implementer has no other copy)

- Each pill gets a 20px avatar of `addedBy` at its right (initials on the member's ramp colour,
  name in the accessible label "Bananas, added by Ola 8 days ago").
- Fresh items 7 or more days old: pill `bg-accent-100`, `text-accent-900`.
- Nudge card above the groups when any are old: `bg-accent-100`, 22px radius, padding 12px 14px,
  13px `text-accent-900`, `Clock` icon: "Bananas and spinach are a week old." and a link "Find
  something to use them" to `/search?p=bananas,spinach`. Dismissible for the day.
