## Context

`formatUnit` (`lib/units.ts` lines 316-322) pluralises any unit whose canonical form is in
`PLURAL_UNITS` (`cup`, `pint`, `quart`). `pt` and `qt` canonicalise to `pint` and `quart`,
so they render as "pts" and "qts", which breaks the file's own rule, stated in the comment
above `PLURAL_UNITS`: "words take an s, symbols do not".

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `formatUnit` pluralises only when the unit as written is the singular word form (`cup`, `pint`, `quart`, case-insensitive); abbreviations such as `pt` and `qt` are returned unchanged.
- [ ] `src/lib/units.test.ts` adds `formatUnit('qt', 2) === 'qt'` and `formatUnit('pt', 3) === 'pt'`, and the existing cases still pass.

## Out of scope

Conversion, and the amount formatting in RL-025.
