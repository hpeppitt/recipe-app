## Context

The More sheet (RL-100) lists Share, Suggest, Delete and similar. Servings live in the page's
stepper state (display only, RL-102). `useKitchen().addRecipe(recipe, servings)` (RL-213).

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A More sheet row "Add to shopping list" (`ShoppingBasket`) on the recipe page and `/shared/:id` for verified users; signed out and anonymous it opens the sign-in sheet (`{ kind: 'continue' }`); hidden in local-only mode.
- [x] Adds at the current servings; the toast says "Added 6 things to your list" counting lines not already in the pantry, with "View" linking to `/kitchen/list`; adding the same recipe again adds it again (amounts sum; no x2 on the chip, see Notes).
- [x] Failure: "Couldn't add to your list. Check your connection and try again."
- [x] Tests: row visibility, scale passed, toast count, failure.

## Notes

RL-213: useKitchen drops any action started while a kitchen write is in flight (up to 5 s), so disable the kitchen controls while a write runs, or a second tap is silently lost. kitchen is null until the first snapshot (show a skeleton, not an empty list). On a timeout it shows "It may have saved. Check your list before adding again."

Done (implementer). `addRecipeToKitchen(uid, recipe, servings)` in `useKitchen.ts` runs the shared `write` (same errors, one write at a time) without subscribing, and counts lines not in the pantry inside the transaction. The page keeps the stepper's servings via a new `onServingsChange` prop on `RecipeContent`. The x2 on the chip is not possible: RL-216 chips do not count repeats, because lines do not record how often a recipe was added; adding twice does sum the amounts. `/shared/:id` has no More sheet, so it gets no row. Anonymous and unverified users get the sign-up dialog (`continue`), since `AuthModal` closes itself for anyone signed in. Full-list and "It may have saved" copy are shown as the toast for those errors; any other failure shows the ticket's copy.
