## Context

The author decided the 3d sibling carousel replaces `VariationChips` on the recipe page
(`docs/design/organic/BRIEF.md`). Today `RecipeDetailPage` renders
`<VariationChips children={children} />` near the end of the page, where `children` comes
from `useRecipeLineage(recipe)` in `src/hooks/useRecipe.ts` (derived from `useRecipeTree`,
so local and cloud records are merged). `VariationChips` renders nothing when there are no
children.

The author chose on 2026-10-18 that a leaf shows nothing: a recipe with no children renders no carousel section, same as a root with no children.

Change tags: `summariseChanges(diffRecipes(cardParent, card), 3)` (RL-066), where
`cardParent` is the card recipe's own parent in the family. Branch counts:
`collectSubtreeIds(nodes, card.id).length - 1` (`src/lib/tree.ts`). Compare goes to the
"What changed" screen (RL-073), comparing the card with its own parent:
`/recipe/<card id>/changes`.

Design 3d, to copy:

- Section heading `<N> ways people took it` (13px uppercase 0.08em, `text-text-tertiary`;
  N = card count; for N = 1 "1 way someone took it"), then the family name (the root's
  title) in `font-heading` 25px. On the right of the heading row, a 44px round icon button
  with the Lucide `Network` icon (or its inline path if RL-064 has not landed), accessible
  name "Version tree", linking to `/recipe/:id/tree`.
- Horizontal scroll-snap row (`snap-x snap-mandatory`, `scroll-padding` 16px, bleeding to
  the page edges like the old chips row). Cards 290 px wide, `rounded-card` (32px, from RL-062), padding
  17.6px, tints rotating by index: `bg-primary-100` (accent-200), `bg-accent-2-200`,
  `bg-surface-secondary`.
- Card content, top to bottom: emoji (32px) and, top right, an `<n> branches` tag (neutral
  tag; "1 branch"; hidden at 0); the prompt as the headline in `font-heading` 25px, max
  three lines (fallback: the title); `<title> · <author>` 13px `text-text-secondary`
  (author fallback "Anonymous"); change tags as Organic tags (same colours as RL-070);
  then a secondary "Compare" and a primary "Open" button side by side.
- Under the row: pagination dots, one per card, 8px, active dot a 20px accent pill,
  inactive `neutral-400`.
- Under the dots, when the viewed recipe is not the root: a row "The original" with the
  root's emoji and title, linking to `/recipe/<rootId>`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `src/components/recipe/BranchCarousel.tsx` renders per Context; `RecipeDetailPage` renders it where `VariationChips` was. `VariationChips.tsx` is deleted and nothing imports it (`grep -r VariationChips src` empty).
- [ ] Card set: the viewed recipe's children, oldest first; if none, the section renders nothing (leaf or root). `useRecipeLineage` is unchanged.
- [ ] Accessibility: the row is a `region` with `aria-roledescription="carousel"` and `aria-label="Branches of <family name>"`; each card is a `group` with `aria-roledescription="slide"` and `aria-label="<i> of <n>"`. Buttons inside cards are reachable by Tab in order. Dots are buttons named "Show branch <i> of <n>" that scroll that card into view; the active dot has `aria-current="true"`.
- [ ] The active dot tracks the card most in view while swiping or scrolling (IntersectionObserver or `scrollLeft` maths, implementer's choice) and after a dot is pressed.
- [ ] Compare navigates to `/recipe/<card id>/changes`; Open to `/recipe/<card id>`.
- [ ] `src/components/recipe/BranchCarousel.test.tsx` covers: children shown with tags and branch counts; a leaf (no children) renders nothing; "1 way someone took it" copy; Compare and Open paths; dots have names and the active one has `aria-current`; "The original" row present on a variation, absent on the root.
- [ ] `docs/capabilities.md` row "Lineage breadcrumb + variation chips" becomes "Lineage breadcrumb + branch carousel" naming `BranchCarousel.tsx`.
- [ ] A `backlog/QA.md` entry: on a recipe with 3 or more children, swipe the carousel on iOS Safari and Android Chrome (snap, dots follow), Tab through with a keyboard, use VoiceOver to hear "1 of 3"; Compare opens What changed; Open opens the branch; a leaf and a lone root show no section; the network button opens the tree. Update the QA.md standing check that names "variation chips" to say "branch carousel".
