## Context

RL-242 asked for `getRecentCooks` to read up to 500 cooks and RL-245 for `getFamilyCooks` to read
up to 1000. Both implementers lowered the default `max` to 200 to protect the Spark plan's 50k
reads a day, and logged it (backlog/DECISIONS.md, the RL-242 and RL-245 rows). The author decided
on 2026-10-22 to restore the tickets' values: the higher read cost is accepted, and the author
will leave the free plan as users arrive. Leaving the plan is not part of this ticket.
The author also decided that `getFamilyCooks` gets a 12 s timeout, because a 1000-row read can
outrun the 6 s `COOK_SUMMARY_TIMEOUT_MS` that every cook read shares today; `getRecentCooks`
keeps 6 s. And a dated note goes under standing decision 2 in `docs/decisions.md`.

Both functions are in `src/services/firestore.ts` (search `getRecentCooks` and
`getFamilyCooks`); neither caller passes `max`, so the defaults are the caps. The caching that
bounds the cost stays exactly as built: the 6 hour localStorage cache for recent cooks
(`services/storage.ts`, key `recipe-lab-recent-cooks`) and the per-`rootId` session cache of at
most 30 families in `hooks/useFamilyCooks.ts`. The `cooks (rootId asc, createdAt desc)`
composite index serves any limit, so `firestore.indexes.json` does not change.

Checked while grooming: nothing in RL-243, RL-246 or RL-247 depends on 200. `cookedThisWeek`,
`mostCooked` and `familyLeader` (`src/lib/cookRanking.ts`) work on whatever list they get, the
tree copy ("cooked by N", "most cooked") and the Explore and Search copy name no cap, and
`parseRecentCooksCache` has no length check. The only 200s are the defaults, two test
assertions, and comments and docs. A cache written before the change holds at most 200 cooks
until it expires within 6 hours; that is fine.
