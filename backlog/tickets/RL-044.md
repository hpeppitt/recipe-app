## Context

The author wants Terms of Service, a Privacy Policy and a Cookie Policy before the circle
grows, and sign-up will require accepting the terms (RL-047). Changing what the app says
about privacy is on the "What needs a human" list in `backlog/README.md`, so this ticket only
drafts. RL-045 renders the drafts as pages, RL-046 is the author's sign-off.

Every claim must match what the code does. The groomer's starting inventory, to be checked
against the code rather than copied:

- **On the device.** Dexie database `RecipeAppDB` with `recipes` (including `chatHistory`,
  the full generation conversation) and `favorites` (`src/db/database.ts`). localStorage keys
  in `STORAGE_KEYS` (`src/lib/constants.ts`: theme, unit system, device id, anonymous uid,
  previous uids, email for linking, stranded identity, intro state) plus `emailForSignIn`
  written directly by `services/firebase.ts`. Firebase SDKs also keep IndexedDB stores (Auth
  session, App Check token, Installations for Analytics).
- **In Firestore** (`docs/data-model.md`): published recipes, publicly readable, including
  `createdBy` uid and display name and the originating `prompt` (`publishRecipe` strips only
  `chatHistory`); public profiles (display name, avatar, counts); favourites; follows;
  suggestions and reply messages; notifications; `clientErrors` with message, stack, route,
  uid and a 300-character user agent (`services/telemetry.ts`), with no retention limit yet
  (RL-010).
- **Third parties.** Firebase Hosting, Firebase Authentication (email address,
  passwordless email links), Cloud Firestore, Firebase AI Logic sending prompts and chat
  history to Google's Gemini models, App Check with reCAPTCHA v3 (`services/firebase.ts`),
  and PostHog product analytics (RL-053, RL-054), EU host preferred, behind the consent
  banner (RL-052), identifying verified users by uid only. GA4 is removed (RL-058) and must
  not appear in the policies.
- **Cookies versus storage.** The app itself sets no cookies; PostHog sets its own
  cookie or localStorage entry, and reCAPTCHA sets Google cookies. Everything else is
  localStorage or IndexedDB. After RL-052, PostHog loads only after an opt-in, stored locally and changeable in Settings; reCAPTCHA and App Check
  are strictly necessary and are disclosed, not gated.
- **Rights and contact.** What the app can honour today, checked on 2026-10-14: "Export All
  Recipes" in Settings exports local Dexie recipes only; nothing exports cloud data (profile,
  favourites, follows, suggestions, replies, notifications). "Clear All Data" is local-only
  (product decision 5). There is no account deletion, and the rules stop a user deleting
  their own suggestions (`allow delete: if false`), replies (immutable) and notifications they
  sent. So the policy must offer erasure, access and export **by email request to
  `SUPPORT_EMAIL`**, fulfilled by the operator following RL-055's runbook, and must not
  promise self-service until RL-056 (export) and RL-057 (deletion) ship.
- **Jurisdiction (author, 2026-10-14).** Governing law: an Australian state, left as the
  placeholder `[STATE]` for the author. The Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy
  Principles apply, and so do the GDPR and UK GDPR.
- **Minimum age.** 16, the highest GDPR default, so one rule covers every regime.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `docs/legal/data-inventory.md` lists every stored item, where, who can read it, retention, and the file that writes it, each verified by reading or grepping the code. Anything the groomer listed that turns out wrong is corrected there.
- [ ] `src/content/legal/terms.ts`, `privacy.ts` and `cookies.ts` export structured content (title, last-updated date, sections of headings and paragraphs) with no new dependency. Plain strings, so RL-045 renders them without a markdown library.
- [ ] The Terms cover: eligibility and the account model (anonymous accounts bookmark locally only; everything else needs a verified email), user content and the licence to show published recipes to other users, acceptable use including no abuse of AI generation (scripted or bulk requests, attempts to exhaust the shared quota, prompts aimed at harmful output) and no abuse of messaging (spam, harassment or bulk suggestions, replies or notifications), that AI output may be wrong and is not safety, allergy or nutrition advice, suspension and data removal, no warranty, changes to the terms, and contact.
- [ ] The Privacy Policy covers each inventory item: what, why, where, who sees it, and how long. It also states that published recipes and profiles are public, and covers:
  - a lawful basis for each processing purpose (for example contract for the account and published content, legitimate interests for error reports and abuse limits, consent for analytics);
  - data-subject rights under the APPs, GDPR and UK GDPR (access, correction, erasure, export, objection, withdrawing consent, complaint to the OAIC, ICO or an EU authority), each with how it is exercised today, by email per RL-055 where there is no self-service;
  - international transfers to Google (Firebase, Gemini, reCAPTCHA) and PostHog, with the safeguard named for each, or a note for the author where the agent cannot verify one;
  - a retention period for every category, including the current lack of one for `clientErrors` and notifications (RL-010 tracks the `clientErrors` TTL), stated honestly rather than invented;
  - the contact route (`SUPPORT_EMAIL`).
- [ ] The Cookie Policy separates cookies (PostHog, reCAPTCHA) from local storage and IndexedDB, says what each is for, marks reCAPTCHA and App Check as strictly necessary, and describes the consent banner and the Settings control from RL-052.
- [ ] The Terms state the minimum age (16) and governing law as the laws of `[STATE]`, Australia, with `[STATE]` left for the author.
- [ ] Each document states near the top: "Draft. Not legal advice. The operator should have this reviewed by a qualified lawyer before relying on it." The author decides at RL-046 whether that line stays.
- [ ] `src/lib/legal.ts` exports `TERMS_VERSION` (the terms' last-updated date as a string) and the three last-updated dates, with a test asserting each content file's date matches.
- [ ] The PR description lists every claim the agent could not verify from the code (for example Google's current data-use terms for the free Gemini tier, PostHog and reCAPTCHA cookie names and lifetimes) for the author to check.
