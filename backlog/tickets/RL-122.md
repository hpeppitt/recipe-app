## Context

**Author's decision (2026-10-19, /groom):** favourites appear in Saved (RL-121) and the heart
becomes the design's bookmark. Nothing about the data changes: the toggle still calls
`useFavorite(...).toggle` / `useCloudFavorite` exactly as today, with the same sign-in prompt
when signed out and the same hidden control in local-only mode (decision 6).

Design: `docs/design/organic/export/App Prototype.dc.html` (the recipe screen's `rc.toggleSave`
buttons and `saveIcon`/`saveColor` in the script) and `Explore and Recipe.dc.html` (2a). The
spec page is `App Spec.dc.html`, "Original recipe (2a)": "Save, Branch, Suggest open the 5a
sheet when signed out".

After the dependencies land the heart lives in three places, all restyled already: the 2a
header row and the 2b floating controls of `RecipeDetailPage` (RL-100), the `/shared/:id`
banner and footer of `SharedRecipePage` (RL-113, whose footer already has a `Bookmark` "Save"
button for the same toggle), and the favourited marker on Explore rows (RL-098). `Bookmark`
and `BookmarkCheck` are added to `src/components/ui/icons.tsx` by RL-088.

**Token map:** `--color-accent` is `text-accent`, `--color-text` is `text-text-primary`. Light
only: no `dark:` classes. 44px targets.

Values to copy:

- Toggle, off: `Bookmark` icon, `text-text-primary`, 20px in a flat 44px button (19px in the
  2b floating `IconButton`, which keeps its `bg-surface` and `shadow-md`).
- Toggle, on: `BookmarkCheck`, `text-accent` (#c67139 on the cream ground is 3.03:1, which
  meets the 3:1 non-text rule; it is an icon, not text).
- Accessible name "Save" in both states with `aria-pressed` true or false (the design's
  `aria-label="Save"`); it replaces "Add to favorites" / "Remove from favorites".
- Explore row marker: `BookmarkCheck` 14px `text-accent-700` after the title in place of the
  red filled `Heart`, `aria-hidden`, with a visually hidden "Saved" next to it.
- `/shared/:id`: the banner's heart is removed, because the footer "Save" (label "Saved" when
  on, RL-113) is the same toggle; one control per action. The footer button switches its icon
  to `BookmarkCheck` in `text-accent` when on.
