## Context

Written for RL-193's recommendations; regroom if the author chose otherwise. `downscaleToJpeg`
(`services/imageDownscale.ts`, RL-118) downscales to a long side of 1024px and at most 600 KB;
the cook photo needs 800px and 150 KB, so parameterise it rather than copying it. Validation of
type and size on input is `lib/imageAttachments.ts`. The photo is written after the cook (the
rule reads the cook), as a second write; a photo failure must not lose the cook.

### The design (the implementer has no other copy)

Left of the note field, a 96px square tile, 28px radius, 2px dashed `accent-400` border,
`Camera` icon 24px in `accent-700`, name "Add a photo". With a photo picked: the photo fills it
in the `.washed` treatment (desaturated, lifted; reuse the app's washed class or add one to
`index.css` with tokens only) with a 32px remove button. Under the sheet's note line, when a
photo is picked: 12px `neutral-700` "Food only, please. No people or personal details. It shows
on the recipe for everyone."

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `downscaleToJpeg(file, { maxSide = 1024, maxBytes = 600_000 } = {})`; existing callers unchanged.
- [ ] Picking accepts JPEG, PNG or WebP (camera or library via `accept="image/*"` and `capture` not forced), downscales to 800px / 150 KB, and shows the preview; a file that cannot be decoded shows "That photo didn't work. Try another."
- [ ] Save: `logCook`, then `addCookPhoto(cookId, recipeId, dataUrl)`; if the photo write fails, the cook stays saved and the toast says "Saved to your cook log. The photo didn't upload."
- [ ] The photo never goes to analytics, telemetry or Dexie.
- [ ] Tests: downscale options, preview and remove, two writes in order, photo failure path.
- [ ] `docs/capabilities.md`: the "Recipe photos" row stops saying "cut permanently" and points to decision 3's dated entry; Log a cook row mentions the photo.
- [ ] A `backlog/QA.md` entry: on a phone, take a photo in the sheet and save; check the Firestore doc size is under 200000 characters (emulator UI); a large panorama still saves.
- [ ] `npm test`, `npm run lint`, `npm run build` pass.

## Out of scope

Showing the photo on the recipe (RL-206). Cropping.
