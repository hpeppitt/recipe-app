## Context

Product decision 5 in `docs/decisions.md` keeps "Clear All Data" local-only and says the
honest version of a bulk exit is account deletion, which it calls server-side work because it
must clean up documents in other users' scope. Without Cloud Functions (product decision 2),
self-service deletion needs rules changes that let a user remove their own traces:

- anonymising suggestions they made on other people's recipes (currently owner-review-only updates);
- anonymising their replies (currently immutable);
- deleting notifications they sent (only the recipient may delete today);
- deleting follows where they are the one followed.

Each loosens a rule written deliberately (see the "Suggestion replies as a subcollection"
decision on why messages are immutable). Revising a product decision is on the "What needs a
human" list, hence `risk: irreversible`. The author accepted revising decision 5 by approving
this ticket on 2026-10-14, and decided that a deleted user's suggestions and replies on
other people's recipes are anonymised, not deleted, matching RL-055. Until this ships, RL-055's email runbook is the
erasure route. Large on purpose; split by seam (rules first, then the client flow) after the
author decides.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `docs/decisions.md` records the revision of product decision 5 (self-service account deletion is allowed, dated 2026-10-14) as a superseding entry, keeping the original text marked superseded, along with the anonymisation choice.
- [ ] The rules permit exactly these changes, only by the uid concerned, each emulator-probed: an anonymising update on a suggestion or message they wrote (text unchanged, display name to null, uid fields to `deleted-user`, nothing else); deleting notifications they sent; deleting follows where they are followed.
- [ ] Settings gains "Delete my account" for verified users. A `ConfirmDialog` says what is removed and what is not. Confirming deletes:
  - the user's cloud data in the order that keeps the rules satisfiable;
  - the Firebase Auth user (re-authenticating by email link if Firebase requires a recent sign-in);
  - local data, as "Clear All Data" does.
- [ ] A partial failure is reported with what remains and how to finish by email (RL-055).
- [ ] `docs/data-model.md` and `docs/capabilities.md` updated.

## Out of scope

Deleting other users' favourites of the user's recipes (those are the other users' data; the
recipe going away is enough), and anonymous accounts (local-only).

## Verification

Emulator walk-through with two users. Human QA in `backlog/QA.md`: delete a test account on
the preview build and confirm in the console that nothing under its uid remains.
