## Context

Long titles wrap fully (`RecipeHeader.tsx` h1 at ~122 and ~135, `break-words`), so on a branch
the family label ("Banana bread variation", accent-2-700) wraps under a three-line title. The
punch list: "Cap titles at 2 lines with an ellipsis; the full title shows in the hero on
scroll." The hero (230px emoji band on branches) scrolls away, so "in the hero on scroll" is
built as a compact bar pinned to the top once the real title has left the viewport. That
reading is the groomer's; log it in `backlog/DECISIONS.md`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Both h1 variants (original 34px, branch 32px, and the 44px wide sizes) clamp to 2 lines with an ellipsis (`line-clamp-2`), keeping `break-words`. The h1 text node still holds the full title, so screen readers read all of it; add `title={recipe.title}` for pointer users.
- [ ] When the h1 is fully above the viewport (an `IntersectionObserver` on the h1; no scroll listener), a bar appears at the top of the recipe column: `sticky top-0 z-20`, `bg-surface`, `shadow-sm`, padding 8px 14px plus the top safe area, holding a 44px back control (same `onBack`) and the full title in 15px bold `text-text-primary`, wrapping with no clamp. It hides again when the h1 is back in view. It is `aria-hidden="true"` and its back button `tabIndex={-1}` (the real controls stay the accessible ones), so screen readers do not get the title twice.
- [ ] The bar does not appear in suggest mode or while loading. In the wide pane (`inPane`) it pins to the top of the pane column and does not cover the list.
- [ ] Under `prefers-reduced-motion` the bar appears without transition; otherwise a 150ms opacity fade is allowed (`motion-safe:`).
- [ ] Tests: the h1 has the clamp class and full text; with a mocked `IntersectionObserver` reporting not intersecting, the bar renders with the full title; reporting intersecting, it does not.
- [ ] A `backlog/DECISIONS.md` row for the bar reading of "in the hero on scroll".
- [ ] A `backlog/QA.md` entry. `npm test`, `npm run lint`, `npm run build` pass.

## Out of scope

The family label, the hero itself, Back behaviour (history back stays), the More menu.

## Verification

Gates: tests, `ui-reviewer`. Human at 390 and 320px with a 90-character title on an original
and on a branch: two lines and an ellipsis, the family label on its own line; scroll until the
title leaves and check the bar shows the whole title and its back works; scroll back up. Check
at 1024px in the pane.
